About the Author

Anu Kay

Anu Kay is a novelist whose work blends personal reflection, historical depth, and cultural identity. Her stories explore the moments that shape us—both joyful and painful—through themes of heritage, belonging, and transformation. For Anu, writing is a way to heal, connect, and make sense of the world.

She studied Creative Writing, Journalism, and English Literature in Germany and holds a Master's in Computer Applications.
